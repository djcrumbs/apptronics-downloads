Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Apptronics Game Studios ("we", "us") operates the Neon Breaker game ("the App"). This page explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Data We Collect

The App itself collects no personal data. Your high score and premium status are stored locally on your device using the browser's localStorage and are never transmitted to our servers.

Advertising

The free version of the App displays advertisements served by Microsoft Advertising (formerly Microsoft Bing Ads). Microsoft's advertising platform may collect standard ad-serving data including your approximate location, device type, and interaction with ads. This is governed by Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

Purchasing the "Neon Breaker Premium" in-app purchase permanently removes all advertising from the App.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Microsoft Store using the Windows.Services.Store API. We do not receive, store, or process any payment card information. Microsoft's handling of purchase data is governed by Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

Analytics

We use Microsoft Store analytics (provided automatically by the Microsoft Store) to understand aggregate download counts, crash reports, and ratings. This data is anonymised and aggregated; we cannot identify individual users from it.

Children

The App is rated Everyone / PEGI 3. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The App does not include social features, chat, or any mechanism for children to share personal information.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data directly, there is no data for us to provide, correct, or delete on request. For data held by Microsoft in relation to your Microsoft account, please contact Microsoft directly.

Contact

For privacy questions: privacy@apptronics.studio
